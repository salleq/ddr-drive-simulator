# Changelog

## v0.4.0

- **CLAP version.** DDR now also comes as a CLAP plugin (Bitwig, Reaper, FL Studio, Studio One and others). The installers put it in the standard CLAP folder; the zips have it next to the VST3.
- **Seat bass dips.** Every car type has two deep, narrow bass dips (about 80–230 Hz) at the front seats, so some bass notes nearly vanish like in a real car. The Back Seat has them in other places, the Sweet Spot has milder ones, and the Resonances knob makes them deeper or shallower.
- **Distortion that sounds like a car stereo.** At 100 % the door speakers sound thick and overdriven, not broken. The door trim rattles only above that, and the torn-cone scratch only comes with Worn Speakers on.
